YEET BOMB: Delete your data

YEET BOMB, by Blank Canvas Software, uses anonymous accounts with no sign-up. You can ask us to delete the data linked to your player at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. Open YEET BOMB and note your nickname, shown on the home screen.
  2. Email softwareblankcanvas@gmail.com with the subject "YEET BOMB data deletion".
  3. Include your nickname. If you can, also include a bomb code you played (for example BOMB-ABC123), so we can find the right player.
  4. We will confirm by email and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your anonymous player ID and player record (nickname, defuse kits, stats)
  • Your replays
  • Your taunts and other messages
  • Reports you submitted

What is kept

  • Traps and moves that are part of other players' bomb chains are kept, so their games keep working. They are anonymized and no longer linked to you or your nickname.
  • Server security logs (such as IP addresses) kept by our hosting provider are deleted automatically, within 90 days at most.

Uninstalling the app removes the data stored on your device. See the full privacy policy.

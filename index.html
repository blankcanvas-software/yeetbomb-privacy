YEET BOMB: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

This policy explains what information the YEET BOMB mobile game ("the app", "we", "us") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. YEET BOMB is published by Blank Canvas Software. Contact: softwareblankcanvas@gmail.com.

Summary

  • No sign-up. You play with an anonymous account created automatically on your device.
  • We collect only what the game needs: your nickname, an anonymous player ID and gameplay content (traps, replays, taunts).
  • We do not sell your data or collect your precise location, contacts, photos or email address.
  • The game shows optional rewarded video ads, served by Google AdMob, only when you choose to watch one.

Some settings, such as sound preferences and offline practice progress, are stored only on your device.

Public content

Your nickname, traps, taunts, replays and scores are visible to other players, and you may share replays and bomb codes yourself. Do not put personal information in your nickname or taunts. Offensive text is filtered automatically, and players can report nicknames and messages. Reported content may be hidden, and nicknames may be reset or locked.

Advertising

YEET BOMB uses Google AdMob to show optional rewarded ads (for example, "watch an ad to retry"). When an ad is requested, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and ad interactions. This is used to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and, where you have consented, personalize ads. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses data from partner apps.

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before showing personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time from Credits → Ad Privacy Choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads). Ads are limited to content suitable for teens.

Service providers

Game data is stored with Supabase, our database and authentication provider, which processes it on our behalf. Data may be processed in countries other than your own. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, as described above. We do not share your data with any other third party, except where required by law.

Security

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access to the database is restricted by row-level security, so players can only change their own data through controlled server functions.

Retention and deletion

We keep game data for as long as it is needed to run the game, for example bomb histories and leaderboards. To ask for your data to be deleted, follow the steps on our data deletion page or email softwareblankcanvas@gmail.com with your nickname. If you can, include a bomb code you played. We will delete or anonymize your player record and the content linked to it within 30 days. Uninstalling the app removes data stored on your device.

Children

YEET BOMB is intended for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your data. To use these rights, contact us at the email above.

Changes

If we change how we collect or use data, we will update this policy and the "Last updated" date before those changes take effect.

How it works

Three steps and a fuse that's already burning.

  1. 1

    Take the bomb

    A friend yeets you a bomb as a link in any chat, and the fuse is already burning.

  2. 2

    Beat their traps

    Every player before you hid a trap inside: quick mini-games made by real people.

  3. 3

    Build yours and yeet it

    Pick a trap, choose how nasty it is, add a dare for whoever blows up and pass it on.

8 wild mini-games

Weekly Chaos Cards twist every trap: mirrored, faster, tiny targets or lights out.

  • SnipeTap the real bomb in the shooting gallery. Fakes explode.
  • Fix the WiresDrag each wire to the socket with the same colour and symbol.
  • Crack the SafeTap when the arrow is in the green.
  • MinefieldTap every real bomb. One fake and you're done.
  • Beat DropTap each gem as it hits the line and skip the bombs.
  • DashJump across the rooftops and reach the flag.
  • SusOne of them is the impostor. Find it.
  • StackDrop the crates and stack the tower to the flag.

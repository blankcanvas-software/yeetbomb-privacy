YEET BOMB: For parents

Last updated: 17 October 2026

YEET BOMB is a party game for Android phones, made by Blank Canvas Software. Friends pass a cartoon bomb to each other with a code or a link. Whoever takes it has 30 minutes to beat a few short mini-games ("traps") built by the players before them, then builds their own trap and sends the bomb on. This page explains how the game protects younger players and how purchases work. Questions? Email us at softwareblankcanvas@gmail.com.

At a glance

  • For ages 13 and up.
  • No chat. Players under 18 can only send lines from a fixed list.
  • Names of players under 18 are hidden on public leaderboards.
  • "Tick sleeps": for players under 18, bomb fuses and notifications pause from 22:00 to 08:00.
  • Everything for sale is cosmetic. For players under 18, purchases start switched off and spending is capped at €20 per 30 days. You can add a parent PIN.
  • Ads only play when your teen chooses to watch one for a reward.
  • No sign-up: we never ask for an email address or phone number, and we don't collect contacts, photos or precise location.

On this page, "players under 18" also means anyone who hasn't told the game they're 18 or over.

Age 13 and up

The first time the game opens, it asks for an age group: under 13, 13-17, or 18 or over. Players under 13 can't play. We only keep the age group, never a birth date, and we can't check it. Once chosen, the age group can only be changed once a day, and if a parent PIN is set (see below), only with the PIN.

Talking and sharing

  • No chat. When players send a bomb, they can add a short taunt and a "forfeit", a silly dare such as "do 10 push-ups" that nobody has to do.
  • Pre-written lines only. Players under 18 can only pick taunts and forfeits from a fixed list, and they only ever see lines from that list, even on adults' bombs. Adults can type their own short lines, which are filtered.
  • Filtered names. Nicknames and crew names are checked for offensive words, links, @handles and phone numbers.
  • Reactions. After playing someone's trap, players can send its maker a sticker, like "GG". Reactions from players under 18 show a code like "Player-7F3A" instead of their name.
  • Report and block. Every player can REPORT or BLOCK anyone. Players who blocked each other can't take each other's bombs, react to each other or ask each other for clips.

Names and what others can see

  • On public boards (the Hall of Fame, world chains, featured traps, and daily and weekly boards), players under 18 appear as "Player-" plus a 4-character code. Only players who already know them from the game see their nickname: their crew, players right next to them in a bomb (one played the other's trap), and players of the same group bomb.
  • Players in the same bomb's chain see each other's nicknames. Group bombs are shared by link (up to 30 players), and everyone who plays one sees the others' nicknames. So help your teen pick a nickname that isn't their real name, and share codes and links only with people they know.
  • Notifications that a trap "got someone" never name a player under 18.
  • Clips need a yes. Before a trap's maker shares a video of your teen's run, your teen is asked and can say no. Even if they say yes, the name of a player under 18 stays hidden. Videos are made on the phone and shared through its share menu; we don't upload or host them.

"Tick sleeps": quiet hours

  • Nights: for players under 18, from 22:00 to 08:00 their local time, the fuse of a bomb they're holding stops burning and notifications wait until morning. A fuse can be paused for up to 12 extra hours per bomb.
  • School hours (optional): Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 15:00, with the same effect. Your teen can turn this on in Settings.
  • "Pause me" (optional): a break of up to 7 days. Any bomb your teen is holding goes back to the player who sent it, notifications wait, and nobody can send clip requests. Playing a bomb ends the break early. A new break can start 7 days after the last one ended.

To know when it's night, the game uses the phone's time zone. Quiet hours don't pause a bomb your teen has sent that nobody has taken yet; if nobody takes it, it comes back to them and never counts as a loss.

Notifications

Notifications are off unless your teen turns them on. Players under 18 only get notifications about their own bombs and traps (for example "your bomb is about to blow") and clip requests. They never get reminders to come back, crew news or reactions as notifications, and nothing arrives during quiet hours.

Purchases

  • Cosmetic only. Everything for sale changes how things look or adds pre-written taunts and stickers: bomb skins, fuses, explosions, trails, taunt packs, replay frames and the Bomb Pass Premium track. Nothing gives a gameplay advantage.
  • Simple prices. Each item is a one-time purchase that costs from €0.99 to €2.99 in euros (Google Play shows the exact price in your currency before you pay). There are no coins, no loot boxes or random items and no subscriptions, and the Bomb Pass never expires.
  • Off until switched on. For players under 18, purchases start switched off. When your teen tries to buy something, the game first shows a real-money check that explains items cost real money and asks them to check with a parent or guardian. Purchases only turn on if they choose to switch them on.
  • Monthly limit. Players under 18 can spend up to €20 per rolling 30 days, counted at the items' euro list prices. It can be set to €0, €10, €20 or €50.
  • Parent PIN (optional). In Settings → Purchases you can set a 4-digit PIN. Once it's set, it's needed to turn purchases on, change the monthly limit, change or remove the PIN, or change the age group; turning purchases off never needs it. A profile with a PIN keeps the under-18 limits even if it says it's 18 or over. We only store a scrambled version of the PIN (a salted hash). After 5 wrong tries in 15 minutes, the PIN is locked for up to 15 minutes, and after 10 wrong tries in a day, for up to 24 hours.
  • Without a PIN, your teen can change these settings themselves, so we recommend setting one yourself. The PIN belongs to the game profile on that phone: deleting the account or reinstalling the game starts a new profile without it.
  • Google Play's own controls. For a lock that stays in place, use Google Play: in the Play Store app's settings you can require a password or fingerprint for every purchase, and with Google Family Link you can approve purchases before they go through. Nothing unlocks in the game until Google Play confirms the payment.

Purchase history, restore and refunds

  • History. Settings → Purchases lists every purchase: the item, its euro list price, the date, the Google order number and its status (for example pending, completed or refunded). Your Google Play order history shows them too.
  • Restore. After reinstalling the game, tap RESTORE in Settings → Purchases, with Google Play signed in to the same Google account that paid, to bring the purchases back.
  • Refunds. Refunds follow Google Play's refund policies and the law. You can ask Google Play, or email us with the Google order number (it starts with "GPA"). Refunded items are removed from the game.

Ads

YEET BOMB only has rewarded ads: an ad plays only if your teen taps to watch one for a reward, such as retrying a trap, and rewards are limited to 5 a day. For players under 18, ads are never personalised and are limited to content rated for teens.

Data and deleting the account

There's no sign-up: the game uses an anonymous account. We never ask for an email address or phone number, and we don't collect contacts, photos or precise location. What we store (nickname, game content, age group, time zone, settings and purchase records) is explained in our Privacy Policy. To delete the account, open Settings → Delete my account in the game, or follow the steps on our data deletion page.

Contact

Questions, worries or a refund request? Email softwareblankcanvas@gmail.com. For anything about a purchase, please include the Google order number (it starts with "GPA").

YEET BOMB: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

This policy explains what information the YEET BOMB mobile game ("the app", "we", "us") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. YEET BOMB is published by Blank Canvas Software. Contact: softwareblankcanvas@gmail.com. Parents and guardians can also read our short page for parents.

In this policy, "players under 18" also means anyone who hasn't told the game they are 18 or over.

Summary

  • No sign-up. You play with an anonymous account created automatically on your device.
  • We collect only what the game needs: your nickname, an anonymous player ID, your age group and time zone, gameplay content (traps, replays, taunts, reactions) and, if you buy something, your purchase records.
  • We do not sell your data or collect your precise location, contacts, photos or email address.
  • Purchases are optional and cosmetic. Google Play handles the payment, so we never see your card or other payment details.
  • The game shows optional rewarded video ads, served by Google AdMob, only when you choose to watch one.
  • Players under 18 get extra protections: names hidden on public boards, quiet nights, fewer notifications and purchase controls.
  • If you use Google Play Games, the game sends your achievements and leaderboard scores to it, and we keep your Play Games player ID with your account so a new phone or a reinstall brings your account back by itself.

Purchases and spending controls

Everything for sale in YEET BOMB is cosmetic, and every purchase goes through Google Play. Google Play handles the payment, so we never see your card or other payment details.

  • Checking purchases. When you buy something, the game sends the purchase token to our server. Our server asks Google Play about it and only then unlocks the item and confirms the purchase with Google Play. A pending payment (for example, one waiting for a parent's approval) unlocks nothing until Google Play says it's complete.
  • Notices from Google Play. Google Play also tells our server directly, through Google Cloud, when a purchase is completed, canceled, refunded or charged back, and once a day our server asks Google Play for refunded orders. Refunded or charged-back items are removed from your Locker.
  • Purchase history and restore. Settings → Purchases shows your purchases (item, euro list price, date, Google order number and status). RESTORE moves purchases made with your Google account to the player you're using now, for example after a reinstall, following the rules in our Terms of Use.
  • Spending controls. Players under 18, and any profile with a parent PIN, start with purchases turned off and a limit of €20 per rolling 30 days, counted at the items' euro list prices (pending and completed purchases count). Purchases only turn on after a real-money check in the game. If a parent PIN is set, it is needed to turn purchases on, change the limit, change or remove the PIN, or change the age group. If a purchase reaches us while purchases are off or over the limit (for example from an older version of the game), we don't unlock it and we refund it. Players 18 or over without a PIN have purchases on and no limit unless they set one.

Time zone and quiet hours ("Tick sleeps")

The game sends us your phone's time zone. If it hasn't, we may guess it from your country. We use it only to work out your local time for these quiet hours:

  • Nights, for players under 18: 22:00 to 08:00 your time, every day.
  • School hours, optional for anyone: Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 15:00, if you turn them on.
  • "Pause me", optional for anyone: a break of up to 7 days. Starting it hands any bomb you're holding back to the player who passed it to you. Taking or playing a bomb ends it early, and a new break can start 7 days after the last one ended.

During night and school hours, the fuse of a bomb you're holding stops burning (for at most 12 extra hours per bomb). During all quiet hours, notifications wait until the quiet time is over, and while you're on "Pause me" nobody can send you a clip request. Quiet hours don't stop the timer of a bomb nobody has taken yet. Players outside a bomb's chain can't see whether its holder is in quiet hours. If we don't know your time zone, night and school hours can't apply.

Notifications

Notifications are off unless you turn them on, and you can turn them off any time in Settings. Players under 18 only get notifications about their own bombs and traps (a bomb came back to them or is about to blow, they won a group bomb, their trap got someone) and clip requests: never reminders to come back, crew news or reactions. Notifications never name a player under 18 whose run failed your trap, and a clip request sent to a player under 18 doesn't say who asked. Notifications more than a day old, or about a bomb that has moved on, are dropped instead of being sent late.

Social features

  • Reactions and stickers. After you play someone's trap, you can send its maker one sticker, like "GG" (up to 100 a day). When you send a bomb, you can also put a sticker on it for everyone in that bomb to see. Players who blocked each other can't react to each other.
  • Chaos Card. Each week, one rule twist applies to new traps. You can vote for next week's card. We keep votes for 10 weeks.
  • Tick Live. On Saturdays (UTC), every bomb defused counts toward a world goal. We note who played that day, so everyone who did gets the reward if the goal is reached. We keep this for 60 days.
  • Group bombs. A group bomb is shared by link: up to 30 people can play its trap once each, for 12 hours (1 hour for hot bombs). We store each run (start and finish times, whether you survived, time left) and its replay. Everyone who played or threw a group bomb sees the others' nicknames on its board; anyone else only sees "Player-" codes.
  • Play solo. The solo campaign, bot rivals and practice keep their progress (levels, stars, rival records) on your phone, with a backup on our server (see Progress backup above); the XP you earn in them counts toward your level like any other XP. ENDLESS RUN sends each finished run (score, traps cleared and when) to our server for its weekly and all-time boards.
  • World bombs (18+ only). If you're 18 or over, you can send a bomb you just armed "to the world", and take a bomb that a stranger sent. Whoever takes it joins the bomb's chain, so, as with any bomb, you see each other's nicknames. Only bombs whose whole chain is 18 or over can go to the world, and players who blocked each other, or anyone in the chain, are never matched. We note which bombs were sent to the world.
  • Clips, only with consent. The maker of a trap can ask the player in a replay for an OK before sharing a video of that run. The player can say yes or no; we store the request and the answer. If you're 18 or over, your name only appears if you said yes. If you're under 18, it stays hidden even if you said yes. Nobody can ask you while you're on "Pause me" or if either of you blocked the other, and a maker can ask at most 20 times a day. Videos are made on the phone and shared through its share menu: we don't upload or host them.
  • Bomb Story, bomb cards and recaps. The story of a bomb, your collectible bomb cards, your weekly recap and "Bomb Wrapped" (your year, from 1 December) are made from game data we already have. They show skill and fun stats, never time played, and we don't collect anything extra for them.

Public content

Your nickname, traps, the look you wear (your cosmetic items), stickers, taunts, replays and scores are visible to other players, and you may share replays and bomb codes yourself. Players in the same bomb's chain see each other's nicknames, and anyone who looks at a bomb can see the country each of its traps was made in. On public boards (the Hall of Fame, world chains, featured traps, daily and weekly boards, and ENDLESS RUN boards), and to anyone outside a bomb's chain, players under 18 appear as "Player-" plus a 4-character code (for example "Player-7F3A"), unless the viewer already knows them from the game (the same crew, right next to each other in a bomb, or the same group bomb). Reactions from players under 18 show this code too. If you bought the Supporter pack and you're in the Hall of Fame, a SUPPORTER tag appears next to your name. Do not put personal information in your nickname or taunts. Offensive text is filtered automatically, and players can report nicknames and messages. Reported content may be hidden, and nicknames may be reset or locked. On Google Play Games leaderboards, who can see your Play Games name and scores depends on your Play Games profile visibility settings.

Advertising

YEET BOMB uses Google AdMob to show optional rewarded ads. You only see one if you choose to watch it for the reward shown: retry a trap, a free defuse kit, ×2 XP for a bomb you just defused, or trying on a shop item for your next 3 bombs (other players see it on your traps while it lasts). Rewards are limited each day (UTC): 3 retries, 1 kit, two ×2 XP rewards and 1 try-on, and 5 in total. Before giving a kit, ×2 XP or a try-on, our server checks Google's signature on AdMob's confirmation (see "Rewarded-ad confirmations" above). We keep the details of each reward for 60 days.

When an ad is requested, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and ad interactions. This is used to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and, where you have consented, personalize ads. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses data from partner apps.

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before showing personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads). Ads are limited to content suitable for teens.

Service providers

Game data is stored with Supabase, our database and authentication provider, which processes it on our behalf. Data may be processed in countries other than your own. Payments are handled by Google Play: our server checks purchases with Google Play and receives purchase notices from it through Google Cloud. Notifications are delivered by Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google). Ads are provided by Google AdMob, as described above. Achievements and Google Play leaderboards are provided by Google Play Games services when you're signed in to Play Games. We do not share your data with any other third party, except where required by law.

Security

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access to the database is restricted by row-level security, so players can only change their own data through controlled server functions. Purchases are checked with Google Play on our server before anything unlocks. A parent PIN is stored only as a salted hash, and wrong guesses are limited: 5 wrong tries in 15 minutes lock it for up to 15 minutes, and 10 in a day lock it for up to 24 hours.

You can delete your account in the app: Settings → Delete my account. This removes right away your nickname, taunts and forfeits, scores, blocks, crew membership, push token, events, reactions you sent or received, clip requests and answers, Chaos Card votes, Tick Live entries, ENDLESS RUN scores, your Play Games link and progress backup, rewarded-ad records, XP and level, unlocked items, Bomb Pass progress, spending settings and parent PIN, and your time zone and quiet-hours settings. You can also follow the steps on our data deletion page or email softwareblankcanvas@gmail.com with your nickname. If you can, include a bomb code you played. We will delete or anonymize your player record and the content linked to it within 30 days. Uninstalling the app removes data stored on your device. Achievements and scores sent to Google Play Games are kept by Google under your Play Games profile, and deleting your YEET BOMB account doesn't remove them; you can delete them from your Play Games profile settings or your Google Account.

Children and teens

YEET BOMB is for users aged 13 and over. The game asks for an age group before anything else, and players who say they are under 13 cannot play. We don't verify ages. Players under 18:

  • can only send, and only see, pre-written taunts and forfeits, never free text;
  • appear as "Player-" codes on public boards to players who don't know them from the game;
  • have bomb fuses and notifications paused from 22:00 to 08:00 their time;
  • only get notifications about their own bombs and traps, and clip requests;
  • start with purchases turned off, with a €20 limit per rolling 30 days and an optional parent PIN;
  • get non-personalised ads limited to teen-appropriate content;
  • can't use world bombs (bombs swapped with strangers), which are only for players 18 and over.

Parents and guardians can find more on our page for parents. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Legal bases

We process game data, including purchases, rewarded-ad rewards, social features and your time zone, to provide the game and the features you use (performance of a contract). We keep purchase records to meet our accounting and tax obligations (legal obligation). We use activity events and invite counts to improve the game, and purchase checks, the Google Play account code and daily limits to prevent fraud and abuse (legitimate interests). We apply the protections for players under 18 to keep younger players safe (legitimate interests). Personalised ads are only shown with your consent (adults in the EEA/UK, through Google's consent form). Push notifications are only sent if you turn them on.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your data. To use these rights, contact us at the email above. You can see your purchases in Settings → Purchases. You can also complain to your data protection authority; in Spain this is the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (aepd.es).

Changes

If we change how we collect or use data, we will update this policy and the "Last updated" date before those changes take effect.

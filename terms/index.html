Who can play

You must be 13 or older. If you are under 18, check with a parent or guardian before buying anything. Parents and guardians: see our page for parents.

Be a good bomber

YEET BOMB is about friendly rivalry. You must not use nicknames, crew names, taunts, forfeits, stickers or reactions to:

  • bully, harass, threaten or humiliate anyone, or encourage self-harm;
  • post hateful, sexual, violent or illegal content;
  • ask for photos, personal details, phone numbers or social-media handles, or arrange to meet;
  • pretend to be someone else, spam, or advertise;
  • dare anyone to do something dangerous, illegal or that could hurt them or others.

Forfeits are just for fun: nobody has to do a forfeit, and you should never do one that feels unsafe or uncomfortable. Players under 18 can only send pre-written taunts and forfeits.

Only share a video of another player's run if they said yes when you asked in the game.

Don't cheat: no modified apps, scripts or bots, and no faking results or leaderboards.

Reporting and blocking

You can report or block any player from the game (REPORT / BLOCK). Reported text is hidden from you immediately. Blocking also stops you and that player from taking each other's bombs, playing each other's group bombs, reacting to each other's traps and asking each other for clips. We review reports and may hide content, reset nicknames, mute players or delete accounts that break these rules, without notice.

Your content

You keep ownership of the traps and messages you create, but you give us a free, worldwide licence to store, show and share them inside the game and in game replays, so the game can work. Traps you've passed on stay in other players' bomb chains even if you delete your account (without your name or text).

Purchases

YEET BOMB is free to play. You can buy optional items through Google Play:

  • Cosmetic only. Everything for sale changes how things look, or adds pre-written taunts and stickers: skins, fuses, explosions, trails, taunt packs, replay frames and the Bomb Pass Premium track. Nothing for sale gives a gameplay advantage: it never makes traps easier, gives you more time or earns you more XP.
  • One-time purchases. Each item is bought once and kept. There are no subscriptions, no coins or other in-game currency, and nothing random.
  • Prices and payment. Google Play shows the price in your currency before you pay, and handles the payment under its own terms.
  • Delivery. We check every purchase with Google Play before unlocking it. A pending payment (for example, one waiting for a parent's approval) unlocks once Google Play confirms it. If something you paid for doesn't appear, tap RESTORE in Settings → Purchases, or contact us with your Google order number (it starts with "GPA").
  • Refunds. Refunds follow Google Play's refund policies and the law where you live. You can ask Google Play or contact us. If a purchase is refunded or charged back, its items are removed from your Locker.
  • Your items stay with your player. Purchases belong to your YEET BOMB player profile and to the Google account that paid. Reinstalling the game or clearing its data gives you a new profile: with the same Google account signed in to Google Play, tap RESTORE in Settings → Purchases to move your purchases to it. A purchase can move to a new profile up to 3 times in 30 days. It can always move if the old profile was deleted, if it goes back to the profile that bought it, or if it goes back to a profile that had it before. If RESTORE can't move a purchase, contact us.
  • Deleting your account. Deleting your account removes your items from that profile. Purchase records are kept (see the Privacy Policy), so with the same Google account you can restore them to a new profile later.
  • No cash value. Items and rewards have no cash value and can't be sold, traded or given to other players.

Players 18 or over can also turn purchases off, set a monthly limit or set a PIN.

The Bomb Pass

  • Each Bomb Pass season has tiers (20 in Season 1) that you fill with Pass XP by playing. The free track is for everyone; the Premium track is a one-time purchase.
  • Seasons never expire. Your progress and rewards stay, and you can unlock Premium any time, even after a season is over.
  • When you unlock Premium, every premium tier you have already reached unlocks at once, and tier 1 straight away.
  • You can't buy tiers, tier skips or XP. Pass XP only comes from playing; the optional ×2 XP ad doubles the XP from one bomb you defused.
  • If a Premium purchase is refunded, its premium items are removed.

Rewarded ads

Ads are always optional. You only see one if you choose to watch it for the reward shown: retry a trap, a free defuse kit, ×2 XP for a bomb you just defused, or a try-on of a shop item for your next 3 bombs (other players see it on your traps while it lasts). Kits, ×2 XP and try-ons are given only after Google AdMob confirms the ad to our server. Rewards are limited each day (UTC): 3 retries, 1 kit, two ×2 XP rewards and 1 try-on, and 5 in total.
